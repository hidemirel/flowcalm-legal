Three things FlowCalm does differently

STUCK ON A TASK? BREAK IT DOWN.

Tap once and any task splits into steps small enough to actually start. No more staring at "write the report" for twenty minutes.

MISSED A DAY? YOUR STREAK SURVIVES.

Streak shields protect your progress from one bad day. FlowCalm never punishes you for being human — no guilt copy, no broken streaks, no reason to give up and delete the app.

TIME DISAPPEARS? WATCH IT DRAIN.

A real visual timer — a ring that empties as your sprint runs — keeps time visible instead of an abstract number ticking down.

Frequently asked questions

Is FlowCalm free to use?

Yes. The core focus-sprint loop, your first ambient sounds, your streak, and your forest are free forever. FlowCalm PRO removes the daily caps and adds unlimited task breakdowns, the full sound library, and your complete stats history.

Do I need to create an account?

No. FlowCalm has no login, no account, and no sign-up. All your data — tasks, streaks, sessions — stays locally on your device.

Does FlowCalm work offline?

Yes, fully. Every feature works without an internet connection.

What is FlowCalm's "body double" mode?

It's a local ADHD focus technique — name a friend or choose a virtual partner and it feels like someone is working alongside you. There is no networking, no account, and no real other person involved.

How do I cancel FlowCalm PRO?

Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from your Apple ID subscription settings. FlowCalm never charges you directly and never sees your payment details.

Is FlowCalm a medical or diagnostic tool?

No. FlowCalm is a self-help focus and productivity app. It does not diagnose or treat ADHD or any condition and makes no medical claims.
