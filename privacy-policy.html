Privacy Policy

FlowCalm Last updated: March 12, 2026

1. Data We Collect

FlowCalm stores all user data locally on your device. No personal data is sent to our servers.

  • Username (chosen by you)
  • Focus sprint data (duration, completion status)
  • XP, streak, and task data
  • Energy level preference

2. Data Sharing

Your data is never shared with third parties. All data remains on your device.

3. Subscriptions & Payments

PRO subscription transactions are processed through the Apple App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Manage your subscription through your Apple device settings.

4. Notifications

With your permission, the app sends local notifications (streak reminders, task reminders). These notifications are generated on your device — no server is used.

5. Analytics

Anonymous usage statistics may be collected to improve the app experience. Your personal information is not included in this data.

6. Children's Privacy

FlowCalm is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data via Profile > Reset Data within the app. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.

8. Contact

Questions? Reach us at support@hidstech.com
